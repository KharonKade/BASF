About the Skatepark

The Baguio Skatepark is designed to accommodate BMX riders, skateboarders, and inline aggressive skaters of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned pro or just getting started, our park is the perfect place to hone your skills.

Park Features

  • 2x Quarter pipe
  • 2x Kicker ramp
  • Rails (low & high)
  • Ledges (low & high)
  • 2x Flat bank
  • Funbox

Park Rules

  1. Skate at your own risk
  2. Skate park hours 9am till 8pm
  3. Keep the park clean and dispose trash properly
  4. Helmets, pads, safety gears are strongly recommended
  5. Be aware and courteous of other riders
  6. Keep all gums, foods, drinks and glass out of the use area
  7. No fighting, vandalism, weapons or profanity
  8. No drugs, tobacco products, e-cigarettes, and alcohol on or around the premises
  9. Parents are encouraged to be in attendance at all times if user is under the age of 10
  10. Violation of these rules may result in suspension from use of the facility
